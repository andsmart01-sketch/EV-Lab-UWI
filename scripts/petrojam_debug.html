      About Us

      Petrojam Limited is Jamaica’s only petroleum refinery. We have over 38 years of industry experience producing petroleum products of the highest quality, to satisfy the local and export market. Here at Petrojam, we are committed to fulfilling our role in advancing Jamaica’s energy security. We take pride in our proven track record of supply reliability, always ensuring that the market is adequately serviced.
