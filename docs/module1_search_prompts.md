# Module 1: search prompts for the 16 unconfirmed actions

Paste these into Google. Each item has a **site-restricted** query (most likely to
find the official document), a **broad** query (catches news coverage and PDFs
hosted elsewhere), and where useful a **filetype** query.

A negative result is a finding. If a targeted `site:` search of the responsible
agency returns nothing, that is evidence the document is not published, which is
stronger than "not found" and can be written up as such. Note the date you
searched.

Useful domains:
`mset.gov.jm` ministry · `mtm.gov.jm` transport/ITA · `bsj.org.jm` Bureau of
Standards · `nepa.gov.jm` environment · `nswma.gov.jm` waste · `our.org.jm`
utilities regulator · `jca.gov.jm` customs · `tradeboard.gov.jm` Trade Board ·
`ta.org.jm` Transport Authority · `jis.gov.jm` government news ·
`japarliament.gov.jm` parliament · `pioj.gov.jm` Planning Institute ·
`statinja.gov.jm` statistics · `mof.gov.jm` finance · `jutc.gov.jm` buses

## Two searches worth doing first

These two would resolve the most at once.

```
site:pioj.gov.jm "Economic and Social Survey Jamaica" 2024 OR 2025 filetype:pdf
```
The ESSJ is where the 575,041 fit-certified and 34,928 PPV figures came from. A
newer edition updates both denominators at once, and its transport chapter may
break out EVs separately.

```
site:bsj.org.jm electric vehicle
```
BSJ is named in three separate actions across Goals 1, 2 and 3. One search
settles whether any EV standard exists.

## Recording what you find

For each item note: date searched, query used, and either the URL found or
"no result". A dated negative from an official site is citable in the report as
evidence of non-publication. Add anything found to `ACTIONS` in
`dashboard/module7_policy.py` with an `evidence_url`.
